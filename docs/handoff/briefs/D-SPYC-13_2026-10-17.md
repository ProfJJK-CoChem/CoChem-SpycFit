### Question
When a predicted dipole or transition-moment component is small, should SPYFY only label and de-prioritise the affected lines, and should the label come from a component threshold or from predicted intensity?

### Context
- **Method Matrix §6.1** (`CoChem-BASE/Method_Matrix.md` l.658 "signed, in the principal axis system"; l.668): "Any conformer with |μ_α| < 0.1 D on an axis is flagged **dark** in that selection rule and is excluded from the predicted line list while being retained in the thermodynamic ensemble."
- **TORQ contract §3.4** (`CoChem-TORQ/docs/wiki/Method_Matrix_Implementation_Contract.md` l.125): "A 0.1 D display/detection threshold cannot delete nonzero archival transitions". Its review (`review/Method_Matrix_Review.md` l.118) calls 0.1 D "a user display threshold, not a physics darkness criterion" and asks for signed PAS components in a fixed right-handed convention; a label uses |μ_g|, the sign kept as SF-CMP-003a records it (SRS l.1093-1094).
- **Three cases.** (a) Zero by symmetry: panel members SO₂ and H₂CO are C₂v, propyne C₃v (https://cccbdb.nist.gov/exp2x.asp?casno=7446095, casno=50000, casno=74997; read 2026-10-17). (b) Weak but nonzero: camphor |μ_c| = 0.0804(7) D, "All three types of allowed dipole moment transitions, μa, μb, μc, were observed" (Kisiel et al. 2003, doi:10.1039/B212029A, abstract). (c) Missing: a dipole absent from `.int` adds no SPCAT strength and zero-strength lines are skipped (`calcat.c` l.645-651, l.666-667; manifest `docs/handoff/calpgm_sources_2026-10-11.sha256`). TORQ distinguishes zero from missing (TORQ SRS l.15, l.290); `AGENTS.md` l.50-56 allows no default without a PI record.
- **Species and axes.** A component zero by symmetry in the parent need not vanish in an asymmetrically substituted isotopologue, whose principal axes rotate (UNVERIFIED here; standard result), so labels are per species, conformer and axis system, recorded with frame and axis convention (SF-PHYS-001a l.521-523; SF-CMP-002 l.1075-1076).
- **Computed dipoles err by about the threshold.** For two n-butanol conformers, equilibrium cam-B3LYP components miss jet Stark values by 0.10-0.32 D, vibrationally averaged MP2 by 0.006-0.13 D (Kisiel and Habdas 2023, doi:10.3390/molecules28041692, Table 3). CO (panel; SRS l.1533) has μ = 0.112 D (https://cccbdb.nist.gov/exp2x.asp?casno=630080&charge=0, Nelson, Lide and Maryott 1967) and μ_a = 0.11011 D in CDMS (https://cdms.astro.uni-koeln.de/cgi-bin/cdmsinfo?file=e028503.cat, Goorvitch 1994), 0.01 D above a 0.1 D cut.
- **Who computes the label.** SPCAT cuts by MIN.LOG.STR (`calcat.c` l.140-141, l.669-679); SF-CMP-002a lists the cutoffs, SF-CMP-002b makes the native model (`docs/decisions/D-SPYC-18.md`) apply them, SF-CMP-002c counts the losses (l.1080-1088). The PI wrote that SPYFY's main value is to "utilize GPU to parallel, array, and make fitting and calculations dynamic recalculate on the fly" (`docs/decisions/D-SPYC-04.md`). So the label comes from whichever producer's intensities are shown and in an array engine is a mask column, never a deletion. Under D-SPYC-16 D "consumed TORQ, BASE and user catalogs keep their producer label" (`docs/decisions/D-SPYC-16.md` l.17-18); this brief proposes labels beside, not inside, them.
- **Settings and modalities.** D-SPYC-20: "20 prefilled. Research default values from publications" (SF-SET-001..008, l.864-895); 0.1 D comes from the Matrix, not a detection study; a visibility setting re-ranks only and never marks predictions stale (SF-SET-003). C1 fundamentals mix a-, b- and c-type transitions by dipole-derivative components (Wilhelm et al. 2023, doi:10.1063/5.0155054, abstract); TORQ supplies vibrational and Raman predictions (D-SPYC-06; SF-RAMAN-002 l.782-785) but has no exporter (errata row 8, l.22). SF-PHYS-007b (l.779-781) labels uncalibrated intensities, so an absolute floor needs calibration; a relative floor does not. No source names who supplies electronic transition moments for P15 (SF-CMP-004e, l.1102-1105); they stay "not evaluated" pending D-SPYC-06 research.

### Options
A extends the sheet's A with three label states; B is the sheet's B; C is new.
- **A. Component label, three states.** Forbidden by symmetry, weak (|μ_g| below the user setting) or missing, with reasons. Weak lines rank lower; nothing is removed.
  - Pros: simple; says which branches to expect; the TORQ contract holds.
  - Cons: ignores dipole uncertainty and intensity; needs a conformer rule.
  - Risks: a 0.1 D preset labels camphor's observed c-type lines weak.
- **B. Exclude weak branches (Matrix as written).**
  - Pros: smallest candidate sets.
  - Cons: conflicts with TORQ §3.4, errata row 2 and camphor.
  - Risks: real lines never proposed; a computed CO dipole below 0.1 D empties its catalog.
- **C. Visibility as a recorded view.** A's three states plus an intensity floor relative to the strongest predicted line (absolute only with calibration) per summed rotational line with hyperfine components added (as SF-SET-006, l.885). Labels carry source and uncertainty, sit beside the catalog and re-rank only. A conformer below the setting on every axis is labelled, never removed (SF-PHYS-003b, l.746-748).
  - Pros: one rule for every modality and producer; keeps the TORQ contract and expert control.
  - Cons: IR, Raman and NIR labels read "not evaluated" until TORQ exports moments.
  - Risks: an absent dipole, Q or spin weight leaves a label uncomputable; ranking such lines is a default to name.

Sub-answers for A or C: pruning in automated search (never | expert opt-in, recorded | on by default); dipole preset (none | 0.1 D | researched); unevaluated lines (normal | lowest).

### Agent recommendation
C. Stage one equals A plus a no-deletion test; fixing the relative floor now avoids a second rule and holds for native-model and GPU array output. Pruning: expert opt-in, recorded, rerunnable. Dipole preset: none until a published detection value is researched; 0.1 D would mark camphor's observed branch. Unevaluated lines: normal rank, shown "not evaluated" with reason, nothing substituted. Main risk: missing moments, chiefly IR and Raman until TORQ exports them. Reduce it with a test that no SPYFY label, mask or view filter removes a catalog or comparison line or changes an `.int` dipole, on `.cat` files made at test time from hash-verified scratch copies (SF-DATA-001e), camphor added once its ESI (availability UNVERIFIED) is checked.
