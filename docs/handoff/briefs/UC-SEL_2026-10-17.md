### Options
- **A. Rotational core, staged.** Release 1: UC-7, UC-8 (Pickett), WP-PICK round trip, UC-1 with SPFIT as fit of record, UC-9 once GEOM agrees a schema, UC-5 when native terms exist. Requirements: SF-IO/SF-RUN (l.435-475), SF-ING (l.481-498), SF-HITL (l.828-848), SF-FIT-001/004/005 (l.917-978), SF-CMP (l.1069-1341; empty until native terms), SF-OUT (l.1351-1375), SF-API-001 (l.587), SF-RUN-004 (l.1430-1434), SF-IF-002/003/004/005a/005c/007/008 (l.539-575, l.660), SF-UI-007; V-DEMO-1 only.
  - Pros: smallest; SPFIT is the oracle; public panel records.
  - Cons: sets aside the band priority (HANDOFF l.25); V-DEMO-2/3/4 deferred.
  - Risks: a rotational-only observation model needs rework.
- **B. Two tracks, staged.** A's release 1 plus UC-4 as P8 only (SF-VIB-002, SF-SCOPE-004, l.1387-1396). P7 is UNAVAILABLE until PGOPHER or native P6 terms exist, and mode interpretation needs vibrational predictions from TORQ (blocked on its exporter, SF-IF-006) or ORCA (D-SPYC-19), so V-DEMO-4 (l.683-685) stays partial. Release 2: UC-2, UC-3, UC-6, P2-P4, P7, P15.
  - Pros: follows both PI orders as far as engines allow; P8 needs neither SPFIT nor TORQ.
  - Cons: V-DEMO-4 partial; two Phase 4 releases and two G4 sign-offs (plan l.18).
  - Risks: P8 has no Pickett-grade oracle (synthetic recovery only, l.179); PGOPHER rights and build unconfirmed.
- **C. One release, order only.** All nine ship at G4 (plan l.18); the PI sets only the build order. Requirements: every id; V-DEMO-1..5.
  - Pros: no release boundary; every workflow demonstrated.
  - Cons: waits on TORQ's schema, an SF-ML-002 provider, BASE's handoff kind, the GEOM schema and research R1/R3.
  - Risks: nothing usable until all lands.
- **D. Vibrational first, staged.** UC-4 before UC-1; requirements as B with WP-VIB (plan l.63) first.
  - Pros: follows HANDOFF l.25 literally.
  - Cons: SPFIT comparison after band fitting; HANDOFF l.29 may mean first overall (amendment l.152); P7 still needs P6 machinery.
  - Risks: stalls until measured spectra and PGOPHER arrive.
