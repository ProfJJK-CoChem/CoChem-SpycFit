### Question

When computed and experimental inertial defects Δ = I_c − I_a − I_b differ in sign, should SPYFY show and flag it, require an expert-review record before export, reject the candidate automatically, or leave the comparison to GEOM?

### Why it matters now

Lines: SpycFit `80ecfa2`, BASE `7d5b8ac`, TORQ `05bf2b3`, cochem-geom `84d5af4`.
- SRS v2 has no inertial-defect requirement (no "inertial" in `docs/srs/CoChem-SpycFit_SRS_v2.md`; `docs/handoff/PI_DECISIONS.md` l.37, l.248-262). It decides whether the review's "binding" SPYC-DIAG-002 (`docs/wiki/review/CoChem-SpycFit_SRS_Review.md` l.365-367) is written and whether SF-PHYS-003b (l.746-747) covers Δ's sign.
- Plan WP-DIAG (`docs/plan/CoChem-SpycFit_Plan.md` l.40) and the `diag` component (`docs/architecture/CoChem-SpycFit_Architecture.md` l.56) wait on it. Options A-C also need a species-candidate pairing record, absent from SRS v2 (UC-3 l.414).
- Phase 2: the legacy module rejects on sign (`src/cochem_spycfit/physics_core/cochem_inertial_defect.py` l.17-20, l.624-679); seven tests assert rejection or filtering (`tests/test_cochem_inertial_defect.py` l.222-231, 234-239, 242-250, 324-343, 345-363, 366-404, 450-463); removing them needs a PR with your written agreement (`AGENTS.md` l.78-82).

### Options

The sheet (`PI_DECISIONS.md` l.259) recommended A; this brief recommends C (A plus a review record). A here adds typing and an uncertainty rule; C, D are new.
- **A. Diagnostic only, typed.** Compute Δ and P_gg with uncertainties for every constant set, labelled by type, reduction, representation and evidence label (SF-INF-001a/b l.925-931). Flag a same-type sign difference when |Δ| > k·u(Δ) for both values; k is a new tolerance (none for Δ in D-SPYC-14, l.164-166), proposed k = 2. An unlike-type pair (Δ_e = 0 against measured Δ_0, the common case) is displayed unflagged unless a recorded correction (sub-question 3) makes it same-type. Never reject, prune or change acceptance.
  - Pros: Matches TORQ §3.1, errata row 1, HANDOFF l.21; keeps styrene and dimer cases.
  - Cons: Without a correction, floppy species get no flag and −17.8 against planar is only displayed; legacy tests rewritten.
  - Risks: A displayed value is overlooked.
- **B. Binding rejection (Method Matrix §6.4).**
  - Pros: Literal wording; keeps the legacy module and tests.
  - Cons: Overrides the accepted contract and HANDOFF l.21; a same-type variant rests on soft-mode VPT2 values (MM l.701).
  - Risks: Planar structures discarded (Oka Table 1; Zhou 2019); near-zero signs rest on an arbitrary tolerance.
- **C. A plus an export review record.** A flagged sign difference requires an expert-review record before export, as SF-CMP-015b (l.1268-1269); headless (SF-API-001 l.587).
  - Pros: Mirrors HANDOFF l.27; a flag cannot reach a publication unseen.
  - Cons: A new requirement; one more export step for soft-mode planar complexes.
  - Risks: Records become routine clicks.
- **D. A for display; the comparison belongs to GEOM** via the SF-IF-008 payload (l.660-663).
  - Pros: One owner for structural judgment; consistent with D-SPYC-10.
  - Cons: Lost at assignment time (UC-3 matching in SPYFY); GEOM needs a comparison path, a payload reader and your cross-repository agreement.
  - Risks: Diagnostic arrives after assignment decisions.

Sub-questions (errata row 1 covers the sign only): (1) may the expert enable an off-by-default |Δ| window, dropped triples logged (legacy AUTOFIT default 0.5, `cochem_autofit_engine.py` l.274-282; styrene would drop)? (2) may same-type Δ agreement rank candidates, never remove them, reasons shown? (3) may DIAG apply a labelled recorded correction (VPT2 Δ_0 where TORQ supplies B_0; Oka's relation from the lowest out-of-plane frequency, Table 1 Δ_0,calc) so Δ_e becomes comparable?

### Agent recommendation

C: A's typed, uncertainty-aware flag plus the export review record. It follows the TORQ contract and errata row 1 and copies your divergence rule (HANDOFF l.27): planar styrene-type or dimer cases are never rejected; a gross mismatch is not exported unreviewed. Main risk: routine acknowledgement and an unflagged unlike-type pair. Reduce it by a record naming both Δ values with types, uncertainties and any correction, by answering sub-question 3 yes, and by acceptance cases from the published Zhou 2019 constants (set and C_rot stated).
