# Errata and overrides for the SRS review

The review `docs/wiki/review/CoChem-SpycFit_SRS_Review.md` (2026-10-11) used the raw Method Matrix
v4 as its main scientific reference. It cites the TORQ decision register (D08 at l.146, D01-D12 at
l.262, D10 at l.471 and l.605), but it never consulted the **accepted** TORQ implementation
contract, `CoChem-TORQ/docs/wiki/Method_Matrix_Implementation_Contract.md`, which corrects parts
of the Method Matrix. Where they conflict, the accepted TORQ documents win unless the PI decides
otherwise. Line numbers below refer to the review file.

Apply these corrections before copying any proposed text from the review into SRS v2, the
architecture or the plan. Agents may add rows; changing or deleting a row needs the PI.

## Items the review got right that need action elsewhere

Report these to the PI. Do not edit CoChem-BASE, CoChem-TORQ or CoChem-TOPOS from this repository.

- **Method Matrix citation error.** The Method Matrix credits the SO₂⋯HF spin-spin and SO₂⋯DF
  deuterium-quadrupole paper (J. Chem. Phys. 85, 3180, 1986; DOI 10.1063/1.451829) to "Andrews,
  Taleb-Bendiab, LaBarge, Hillig & Kuczkowski". The authors are A. J. Fillery-Travis and
  A. C. Legon. It appears in MM §3.1 (spin-spin row, BASE l.323), §6.3 (SO₂⋯DF χ(D), l.680), §6.6
  (l.705) and reference 15 (l.3686), in both the BASE and TORQ copies (TORQ line numbers +2).
- **User Manual Ch. 5** (`CoChem-BASE/CoChem_User_Manual.md`, from l.742) describes SpycFit as an
  IR/Raman/UV-Vis simulator. It needs revision once D-SPYC-01 and D-SPYC-06 are decided.
- **Agent instructions.** The review recommended replacing `.github/copilot-instructions.md`.
  The handoff did this: the old roster is at `docs/legacy/copilot-instructions_2026-08.md`, and
  `AGENTS.md` now carries the rules.
